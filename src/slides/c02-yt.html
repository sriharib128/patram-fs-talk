Here is the situation. We have a 10 gibibyte image shard. The images inside are JPEGs, so they are already compressed. And a reader wants one image at a time, out of tens of thousands in the shard.

So here is the question. Should we compress the shard file? Option A: compress the whole shard, as you normally would with an archive. Option B: store it uncompressed. Take a moment, and tell me which you would pick and why.

We chose B, for two reasons. First, compression barely helps: the images are already JPEGs, and compressing them again saves 0 to 2 percent. Second, and this is the important one, in an uncompressed TAR file every image sits at a fixed byte position. So reading one image is one seek and one read, with nothing to unpack. And as a bonus, building a shard is plain copying, at 1 to 2 gigabytes per second. That fixed position is the idea the rest of the design rests on.
